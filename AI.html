About the Department

The Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI & DS) Department at Karanjekar College of Engineering & Management is committed to shaping the future of intelligent systems and data-driven innovation. Our curriculum blends core concepts of machine learning, deep learning, data analytics, and statistical modeling with practical exposure to real-world applications. The program is designed to develop proficient AI engineers, data scientists, and research professionals capable of solving complex problems and driving digital transformation across in dustries.
